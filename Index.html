AKWETE PAY · CASE-STUDY ANALYSIS

Evidence and proposed response

Data (case brief): 2,400 merchants in the Onitsha cluster; 312 inactive for 30 days (13%); monthly cluster volume from ₦6.2bn (Oct 2025) to ₦4.9bn (Jan 2026); failed transactions from 2% to 7%.

Root causes (40 of 312 inactive merchants interviewed): settlement delays (18), slow reversals (11), competitor fee or free terminal (7), other reasons (4).

Priority account: Ifeoma Superstores fell from ₦48m to ₦17m a month with three reversals (₦410k) pending.

Proposed interventions: own and resolve reversals first, investigate failure rates, contact every inactive merchant, confirm a first successful transaction, then follow up at day 7 and day 30. Use discounts only with commercial approval.

Proposed targets, not results: reactivate 25% of the cohort (78 merchants) within 30 days as a planning test; recover 80% of the Ifeoma Superstores baseline (₦38.4m).
